How to Add a CNAME to Your Domain

{% if ip %}
  1. Log in to your domain provider account.
  2. Go to your DNS management dashboard (on your domain registrar or DNS provider).
  3. Look for a section called DNS Records, Manage DNS, or DNS Settings.
  4. Add the following TXT record:
    Name/Host Value/Points
    netlify-challenge cpp
  5. Add the following CNAME records:
    Name/Host Value/Points
    www {{ip}}
    @ {{ip}}
  6. Save the changes.

Note: DNS changes may take up to 48 hours to propagate.
